# Licensing

InfoBento is open-source hardware. Different parts of this repository are
released under different licenses, each chosen for the kind of work it covers.
**All three permit commercial use, including manufacturing and selling devices
based on this design.**

| Part of the repo                              | License                                    | SPDX identifier  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Software (`packages/`, `scripts/`, repo root) | Apache License 2.0                         | `Apache-2.0`     |
| Hardware design files (`hardware/`)           | CERN Open Hardware Licence v2 — Permissive | `CERN-OHL-P-2.0` |
| Documentation (`docs/`)                       | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0           | `CC-BY-4.0`      |

License texts:

- [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) — Apache-2.0 (software)
- [`hardware/LICENSE`](hardware/LICENSE) — CERN-OHL-P-2.0 (hardware)
- [`docs/LICENSE`](docs/LICENSE) — CC-BY-4.0 (documentation)

## What this means

- **You may build and sell InfoBento devices** from these design files, modify
  the design, and ship your own product based on it.
- **Apache-2.0** (software) includes an explicit patent grant and requires you
  to preserve copyright/license notices and state significant changes.
- **CERN-OHL-P-2.0** (hardware) is the permissive CERN hardware licence: it
  grants the right to make, have made, and sell products, with no obligation to
  share back your modifications.
- **CC-BY-4.0** (docs) lets you reuse and adapt the documentation as long as you
  give appropriate credit.
